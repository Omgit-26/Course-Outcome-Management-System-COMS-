Outcome-Based Education System

Course Outcome Management System

A streamlined platform to define Course Outcomes, manage student assessment records, and automate direct CO attainment calculations.

📚 Course & CO Mapping

Configure academic courses and map specific measurable outcomes (CO1, CO2, CO3) to each curriculum.

📝 Assessment Setup

Create unit tests, practical exams, and assignments directly associated with targeted Course Outcomes.

✍️ Student Marks Entry

Record student evaluation scores with automatic validation ensuring marks do not exceed maximum values.

📊 Direct Attainment Calculation

Automatically evaluate the 60% benchmark target and calculate CO attainment percentages and performance levels.
